Seed scanner line 2

Replace the manual sampling station on line 2 with an inline CT scanner, so quality data follows the batch instead of being reconstructed afterwards.

Why

Goal
Quality data follows every batch off line 2 within 24 hours of the run, with no manual sampling and no file copying by hand.
Current situation
An operator pulls a sample every fourth pallet and carries it to the lab. Results land two to five days later, when the pallet has usually shipped.
Problem or opportunity
Deviations are found after despatch. Three recalls last season traced back to batches the lab had already flagged, too late to act on.

What

Chosen solution
Inline CT scanner above the belt, with the data landing in the platform automatically.
In scope
Line 2 only. Scanner, mounting, data path, operator training.
Out of scope
Line 1 and 3. Any change to the lab workflow.
Standing risks
Dust ingress in the tube housing. Vibration from the upstream elevator.
